02 · How It Works

Four steps. Raw exports in, owner email out.

Every screenshot below is from the working demo build.

01

Drop in your raw exports

Save the month's sales register and receipts exports into one folder, exactly as Tally or your ERP gives them: report headers, Indian date formats, text-formatted numbers and all.

No reformatting. No pasting into templates.

A raw monthly sales export, as it comes out of the system.

02

One refresh cleans and combines everything

A Power Query pipeline reads every file in the folder, strips headers, fixes dates and numbers, removes duplicates, and joins sales to your dealer, SKU, rep and target masters.

New month? Add the file and refresh. Nothing to rebuild.

Refresh loads all tables: 20,528 sales lines and 5,066 receipts in the demo.

03

A dashboard that answers the owner's questions

Sales vs target, achievement %, collections, outstanding, overdue, 90+ day dues, DSO and receivables ageing, with month and region filters.

Every number is calculated in the data model. In the demo, each total was checked against an independent answer key; on your build, against your current MIS.

The same dashboard filtered to one region. Titles and targets follow the filter.

04

AI writes the summary. You approve it. The owner gets it.

The draft comes to you first. Only after you click Approve does the owner receive the email with the dashboard PDF attached.

06 · Common Questions

What people usually ask first.

Does it work with Tally?

It's built to read standard CSV and Excel exports, like those from Tally and most ERPs. In the free review we look at your actual export format before anything is promised.

Does the owner need Power BI?

No. The owner receives the summary and a PDF of the dashboard by email.

Can the AI get a number wrong?

The AI doesn't calculate anything. It writes sentences around figures the data model has already calculated, and you read every draft before it is sent.

What if my reports look different from the demo?

The demo is a starting point. Your build follows your regions, reps, targets and the KPIs your owner actually looks at.

Is this real client data?

No. Everything on this page is a demo built on a fictional distributor dataset. No client data is ever shown publicly.
