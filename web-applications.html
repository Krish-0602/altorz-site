06 · Common Questions

Everything you probably want to ask on a first call.

Who owns the code?

You do. Full source code, documentation, and database schema are handed over on project completion. No vendor lock-in, ever.

Where is the app hosted?

On your own Vercel and Supabase accounts, under your subdomain (e.g., crm.yourcompany.com). We help you set up both accounts during go-live.

What's the ongoing cost after launch?

Approximately ₹2,000/month combined for Vercel and Supabase hosting (varies with usage). No per-user fees, ever. Optional AMC retainer for support.

What happens after 30 days of support?

Either your team takes over maintenance, or you sign a monthly AMC retainer (₹15,000–25,000/month) for ongoing bug fixes, support, and feature additions.

Can we start small and expand later?

Yes — in fact, we recommend it. Most clients start with Build Starter for one critical workflow, prove the ROI, then expand modules over Phase 2 and Phase 3 engagements.

Do you build native mobile apps for Play Store?

No. Modern web apps work beautifully on mobile browsers and deliver 95% of the value at 20% of the cost and maintenance of native apps. If you genuinely need native, we'll refer you to specialists.

What if we need changes during the build?

Minor refinements during weekly demos are included. Major scope additions become change requests (₹5,000–10,000 each) or get deferred to a Phase 2 engagement.

How is payment structured?

50% advance to kick off, 50% on UAT sign-off. Invoice issued per Altorz standard terms. AMC retainers are billed monthly in advance.
